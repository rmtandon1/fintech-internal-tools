Build or buy · what you'll face

Build it yourselves, one app at a time, with one engineer in charge

Hidden logic

  • Formulas and flows nobody wrote down
  • Devin reads each app's export and lists every formula and flow step, done or to do

A platform to own

  • Login, hosting, backups and monitoring that Power Apps gave you
  • Build them once, on the engine every app shares

Review load and sprawl

  • Every change needs an engineer, and cheap changes invite more of them
  • One owner who can say no. Rules ship off, and come out when they stop earning their place

Why build

  • Internal tools become part of the product: same engine, same review, same owner
  • The ten new apps reuse the roles, rules and audit log the first three already have

Where Devin fits

  • If you build: Devin writes the changes. Your engineers review and merge them.
  • If you stay on Power Apps: Devin can still build custom connectors and write tests and documentation.
